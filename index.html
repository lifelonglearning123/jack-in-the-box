► Section 04 — Workshop detail

What you'll actually get.

Workshop 01

"Getting to Eureka!"

A powerful, energising full-day workshop giving individuals and organisations the information, tools and techniques to produce creative and innovative ideas and solutions.

Participants leave with practical frameworks they can apply immediately to generate ideas, solve problems and drive innovation within their business.

  • Idea generation tools
  • Practical problem solving
  • Team-level innovation rituals
Workshop 02

"From Eureka to the Marketplace"

One of the most critical — and most frequently failed — steps in innovation is taking the 'Eureka moment' and turning it into a viable, market-ready product or service.

This full-day workshop covers flexibility, resilience, persistence and the mindset needed to make mistakes, learn from them and evolve great ideas into real business growth.

  • From concept to commercial reality
  • Resilience & iteration mindset
  • Avoiding the classic 'idea-to-market' traps
Workshop 03

"Start-Up Accelerator"

A distillation of Workshops 1 and 2, specifically designed for potential and new micro business owners, start-ups and their teams.

This full-day session provides the key tools, techniques and processes to help new businesses build creativity and innovation as a core driver from the very beginning.

  • Founder-friendly frameworks
  • Innovation built in from day one
  • Lean, fast, immediately useful
Service 04

"Creative Consultancy"

Our bespoke consultancy service works exclusively with you to build and deliver tailormade training and creative solutions designed around the specific needs of your business.

A truly personalised service — no off-the-shelf solutions. Just expert guidance, creative thinking and practical tools crafted entirely for your organisation.

  • 1-to-1 with your team
  • Bespoke training programmes
  • Strategy + creative direction

Every solution is built for micro business first — but transfers effortlessly to larger organisations.
