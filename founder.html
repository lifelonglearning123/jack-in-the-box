► The story

A career spent
between the boardroom
and the back-room.

Ian's career is unusual in that he has worked at both ends of the business world — leading ventures inside one of the world's largest drinks companies, and championing the UK's smallest employers as a national voice.

That contrast is the foundation of Jack in the Box. The creative and innovation tools that work in a global FTSE giant can work for a 1–9 person business — but only if you strip them back, translate them, and rebuild them around how micro businesses actually operate. That's what these workshops do.

Ian founded Jack in the Box Training Co to put thirty years of that thinking into a practical, full-day format any micro business owner can use the very next morning.
