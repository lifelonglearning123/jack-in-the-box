► The story

A career spent
between the boardroom
and the back-room.

Ian's career is unusual in that he has worked at both ends of the business world. Fourteen years at Diageo, where he formed the Venture team. A decade at the helm of the Forum of Private Business, the national membership body representing over 30,000 private and family firms. And, since 2021, Chairman of the Microbusiness Alliance — the voice of more than 1.2 million UK micro businesses.

Along the way he's also built things himself: he owned a couple of pubs, founded Hunters Gin, and continues to back small companies as a business angel and investor. So he doesn't just talk about the 1–9 person business — he's run them, owned them, funded them and represented them.

That mix is the foundation of Jack in the Box. The creative and innovation tools that work in a global FTSE giant can work for a 1–9 person business — but only if you strip them back, translate them, and rebuild them around how micro businesses actually operate. Ian founded Jack in the Box Training Co to put three decades of that thinking into a practical, full-day format any micro business owner can use the very next morning.
